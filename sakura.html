Overview

Taking inspiration from the beauty and symbolism of the Sakura, the interiors blend together familiar floral patterns, materials, and visual references, creating a cohesive expression that feels rooted in Japanese design while offering a warm and immersive dining experience.

At the entrance, an overhead canopy of cherry blossoms establishes the first impression. The abundance of flowers, timber detailing and warm lighting creates a sense of transition from the bustle of the surrounding street into the restaurant.

Inside, the Sakura theme becomes the central design vocabulary. Warm timber is used extensively across the furniture, wall framing and ceiling grid. The ceiling combines timber grids, woven surfaces and circular illustrated panels, complemented by softly glowing pendant lights.

The walls are treated as a curated gallery of Eastern imagery. A collection of circular illustrations, framed in timber, depicts landscapes, people, flora and scenes inspired by Asian visual traditions. These are interspersed with recessed wooden display niches containing decorative objects, creating rhythm and variation along the dining walls.

The furniture continues the same pattern. Natural timber tables and sculpted wooden chairs are paired with floral upholstery, bringing softness and colour to the predominantly earthy material palette. Shades of blush pink, warm wood and muted natural tones reinforce the Sakura-inspired atmosphere.

The result is an interior that aims to make the journey into the restaurant part of the dining experience, moving from the visual noise of the city into a warmer, more immersive environment where food, culture and interior design come together.
