Overview

Walking into Skintis, you almost forget you're at a traditional skin clinic. The soft blush pinks, sage greens, and warm gold accents make it feel welcoming, as if you're entering a boutique retreat rather than a medical space.

Reception Area and Entrance
Feature wall with photo spot -waiting lounge
Artistic wallpaper Feature
Clinic corridor and Procedure rooms

The reception welcomes you with a backlit gold logo set against fluted glass panels - warm, layered, and quietly luxurious, without trying too hard. The waiting lounge, with its beautiful mural of a woman surrounded by leaves and little koi fish, greets you at the entrance, making the wait feel relaxing even before your consultation begins.

Theme details
Procedure room interiors

As you walk further in, the same calm continues across the corridor of procedure rooms - pastel green doors with gold handles, and a pretty floral ceiling design that catches the light. It just feels like a space we designed to be more than a clinic - one where skin, care, and comfort belong together.
