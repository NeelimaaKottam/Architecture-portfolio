Overview

Villa A-54 is a contemporary G+1 residence with sculpted volumes and a warm stone-and-plaster envelope. Curved corners, timber fins, and deep overhangs give it a strong architectural presence, softened by greenery on pergolas and balconies.

A stone-clad entry with a sculptural pivot door welcomes at ground level, while the rear opens onto a lawn with a lily pond and covered sit-out.

Natural stone flooring runs throughout, paired with classy, layered interiors - wood tones, textured finishes, and quiet detailing; balancing traditional warmth with a clean contemporary shell.
