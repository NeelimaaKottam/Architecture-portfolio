Overview

Designed around the vibrant spirit of a Jatara, the restaurant brings together the colours, energy and cultural character of Telangana within a contemporary dining environment.

Reception Area and Entrance
Feature wall with photo spot -waiting lounge
Artistic wallpaper Feature
Clinic corridor and Procedure rooms

The interiors were conceived as an immersive expression of Telugu food and culture, reflecting the local identity of Hyderabad while creating a space that feels warm, lively and celebratory. Rather than directly recreating a traditional setting, the design interprets familiar cultural elements through materials, craft, artwork, colour and spatial details.

Theme details
Procedure room interiors

The resulting space is intentionally vibrant and informal - a contemporary restaurant that celebrates the character of Jatara through colour, craft, art and atmosphere.
